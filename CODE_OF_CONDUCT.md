# Code of Conduct / Xulq-atvor kodeksi

🇬🇧 [English](#english) · 🇺🇿 [Özbekça](#özbekça)

## English

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
This is a short summary of how we work together.

### Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Many contributors are students and
first-time open-source participants, so we especially welcome beginners.

### Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially to people who are learning.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Critique code, not people.
- Respect privacy. Never post personal data about students, parents, teachers or other users.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.

### Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information (doxxing), including data from the live system.
- Spam, fake accounts, or manipulating project metrics such as stars.
- Deliberately exploiting vulnerabilities against the production site instead of reporting them
  via [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

### Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer: Telegram <https://t.me/husanbek_coder> or a private
message to [@Xusanbek0039](https://github.com/Xusanbek0039) on GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues and may temporarily or
permanently ban participants who break this code.
